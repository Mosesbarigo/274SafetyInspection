I. HOUSEKEEPING
(a) Is the area clean and tidy?
(b) Is the area free from trip hazards?
(c) Is electrical equipment and fittings clean and tidy?
(d) Are bins empty?
(e) Are all corridors unobstructed?
(f) Is the interior surfaces of ceilings/walls free of evidence of mould growth?
II. NOTICEBOARDS
(a) Is the noticeboards updated?
(b) Is the Harmony Safety and Health Policy displayed?
(c) Is the Harmony Environmental Policy displayed?
(d) Is the Harmony Life Saving Rules displayed?
III. PPE
(a) Is the requirement of the use of PPE enforced?
(b) Is the required PPE worn?
(c) When not used, is PPE properly maintained or stored?
(d) Is PPE readily available for all personnel including visitors to the area?
(e) Is PPE in use stored at the right place and not left lying around?
(f) Is PPE in a good condition?
IV. LIGHTING
(a) Are the lighting levels sufficient for the work being performed?
(b) Are there any non-working lights?
(c) If diffusers are fitted, are they clean?
V. FIRST AID KITS
(a) Is the emergency equipment easily identified by signage?
(b) Are the contents within the use by date?
(c) Is the Paramedic Bag Tamper seals intact and not broken?
(d) Is the Paramedic checklist completed? (Do the count and update the checklist only when the seal is broken)
(e) Are the AED case contents intact and in the AED box?
(f) Is the AED checklist updated?
VI. FIRE EXTINGUISHING SYSTEMS
(a) Are fire extinguishers adequate in number and clearly visible?
(b) Are extinguishers free from blockage or obstruction?
(c) Are all fire extinguishers serviced, maintained, and tagged at intervals in line with site standard (6 monthly check)?
(d) Are all fire extinguishers fully charged and at their designated locations?
(e) Are fire extinguishers of the correct type?
(f) Is there adequate number of hose-reels?
(g) Are the hose-reel seals in place?
(h) Has the hose-reel been inspected and is the tag in date?
(i) Are any Fire Hydrants leaking?
(j) Have fire hydrants been inspected and is the tag in date?
VII. FLOORS AND WALKING SURFACES
(a) Are all floors safe, no spills, slippery conditions or trip hazards?
(b) Are all floors & walking surfaces clean, dry, and free of oil, grease or rubbish?
(c) Are all floors safe, no spills, slippery conditions or trip hazards?
VIII. AISLES
(a) Are the aisles marked where applicable?
(b) Are the aisles clear of obstructions, debris, trailing cables and hoses?
(c) Are the aisles clear of obstructions, debris, trailing cables and hoses?
IX. SIGNS
(a) Are mandatory signs applied to warn of hazards?
(b) Are they clean, not damaged and legible?
(c) Are they appropriate for the hazard, area, condition or requirements?
X. ELECTRICAL – CABLING, CONDUIT, PLUGS AND OTHER FITTINGS
(a) Are the electrical cables, conduit, plugs & other fittings free of damage/defects?
(b) Is the RCD sticker applied to distribution board and within test date?
(c) Are the plugs and equipment switched off when unattended?
(d) Are all emergency stop buttons colored red and in working order?
(e) Are buttons on control panels clearly identified and labeled?
(f) Are instrument junction boxes and control panels secured or locked?
(g) Are cable trays rusted / corroded?
XI. EMERGENCY
(a) Are emergency exit lights fitted and operational?
(b) Are emergency exit doors clearly identified and visible with signs on them?
(c) Are smoke detectors clear of obstructions, been tested and are working?
(d) Are all employees aware of the nearest Emergency Muster Point?
(e) Is the Muster Point clear of obstructions and properly sign posted?
(f) Is there an appointed Fire Warden in the area?
(g) Is the Area Warden name and photo displayed on the board?
(h) Is the Emergency Phone number displayed?
(i) Is Emergency Evacuation plan displayed and legible?
XII. CAR PARKING AREA
(a) Is there clear vehicle access to the car park?
(b) Is the car park area clean and free of rubbish and litter?
(c) Does the area provide fundamentally stable parking?
(d) Is there adequate lighting in the car park?

Regulation Definitions (274 & 281)

274. Manager to inform himself of condition of appliances. The manager of every mine, or some other competent person assigned by the manager, shall keep himself constantly informed of the state of all ropes, safety appliances or gear used with the cages or shafts; and at intervals of not more than one week carefully examine the condition of the mine and all buildings, machinery, ropes, safety appliances, gear, shafts, levels, planes and other places used in the working of the mine; and make and sign an entry in the prescribed Mine Register setting out the date and fact of the examination, his opinion of the condition, and the need for alterations or repairs, having regard to the safety of persons employed in the mine.

281. Weekly inspection by Manager. Make a thorough inspection of every part of the mine; and make and sign an entry in the prescribed Mine Register recording the facts existing generally in the mine in regard to safety and ventilation, and in regard to any other matter required by this Regulation or by an inspector in writing to be the subject of the record.
